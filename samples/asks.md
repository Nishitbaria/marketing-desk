# Sample asks

DEMO / SYNTHETIC. Paste one into the studio room. Each starts with `@studio-brief`.

## 1. Launch video

```
@studio-brief launch video for my app. Driftnote goes live on the App Store next week. I want people to get it in 30 seconds.
```

## 2. Explainer

```
@studio-brief explainer for Driftnote: how the weekly recap works, for people who have never journaled. Keep it under 90 seconds.
```

## 3. Faceless short

```
@studio-brief faceless short for TikTok and Reels. Hook on "you don't need a new habit". No faces, just the phone and text.
```

## The 2am ask (the demo)

```
@studio-brief launch video for my app
```

One line. Brief plans all three cuts from it: L (launch), E (explainer), F (faceless short).
