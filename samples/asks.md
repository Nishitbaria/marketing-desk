# Sample asks

DEMO / SYNTHETIC. Paste one into the `studio-studio` room. Driftnote is the fictional product in `brand/brand-kit.md`.

## 1. Launch video

```
@studio-quill launch video for my app Driftnote, 30s, vertical, for people who keep quitting journaling. It goes live on the App Store next week.
```

## 2. Explainer

```
@studio-quill explainer for Driftnote: how the weekly recap works, for people who have never journaled. Keep it under 90 seconds.
```

## 3. Faceless short

```
@studio-quill faceless short for TikTok and Reels. Hook on "you don't need a new habit". No faces, just the phone and text.
```

## Passing

Quill passes an ask when its reply has all six parts in order, stays in the brand tone, lists gaps in Open questions instead of inventing them, and ends with `ready for @studio-keeper brand check`. For example, Driftnote Plus has no price in the brand kit, so a script must not state one.
