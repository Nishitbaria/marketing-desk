## The challenge

Build a workflow in a team chat where at least one AI agent and one person get a real task done together, connected through Switch.

- You don't have to build a chatbot or a new agent from scratch.
- Switch is mandatory. It has to be central to the project, and the README should show how you used it.
- Existing agents, libraries and templates are fine. The Switch setup and the workflow your agents run must be built during the hackathon.
- AI coding tools are allowed. You should understand what you submit and be able to explain how it works.

### Track 3 rules

1. Pick an [open issue labelled `hackathon`](https://github.com/sandbox-quantum/switch/issues?q=is%3Aissue+state%3Aopen+label%3Ahackathon). Only these count.
2. Comment on it, tag `@petr-sandbox`, and say in a line or two how you plan to solve it.
3. Wait until you're assigned. Read `CONTRIBUTING.md`, which covers dev setup and the one-time contributor agreement (CLA).
4. Open your pull request and link the issue with `Closes #number`.

Conditions:

- One person per issue. If it's assigned or someone asked first, pick another.
- Up to 5 open pull requests at a time, each for an issue assigned to you.
- A pull request for an issue not assigned to you won't be judged.
- The pull request must be open by Nov 7, 11:59 PM IST.
- An assigned issue with no activity for 48 hours may be handed to someone else.
- Low-effort pull requests, such as typo fixes, formatting or unreviewed AI output, won't be judged.
- Spam gets you removed from the track.

## Judging

| Weight | Criterion | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **30%** | Use of Switch | Is Switch central, and would the project stop working without it? |
| **25%** | Technical execution | Does it run, and could someone clone the repo and reproduce it? |
| **25%** | Creativity and impact | Is it a fresh idea that solves a problem a real team has? |
| **20%** | Presentation | Do the demo video and README make clear how Switch is used? |

Process: WeMakeDevs reviews every submission and creates a shortlist. Switch, DevTools Academy and WeMakeDevs then judge the finalists.

Judged separately:

- **Best Core Contribution:** the most useful, best-built pull requests.
- **Best Blog:** clarity, accuracy, and how much it helps a first-time Switch user.

## Official rules (summary)

Entering is a binding agreement with Switch AI, the Sponsor.

**Eligibility**

- You must be at least 18, or the age of majority where you live if that's higher, from entry until any prize is awarded.
- No general geographic limit, but the Sponsor can exclude jurisdictions where the event or prizes would be prohibited or restricted.
- Excluded: anyone on sanctions lists (including OFAC, EU, UK and UN lists), or in or ordinarily resident in sanctioned places. These currently include Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and the Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson regions of Ukraine.
- Not eligible to win: employees, officers, directors and contractors of the Sponsor, its affiliates and promotional agencies, judges, and their immediate family and household members.
- If an employer, contract or institution restricts you, you must get permission. Government employees may be ineligible or limited.

**Genuine participation**

- Use one accurate, verifiable identity. Your email and legal name must match prize verification.
- No bots, scripts, macros, automated agents or bulk entry for registering or submitting.
- No multiple accounts, aliases, disposable emails or other people's identities.
- No collusion, vote manipulation or entry stuffing.
- AI tools in the project are fine. This section covers fake or automated participation, not the technology you use.
- The Sponsor can require ID verification, including government photo ID or a live session. Failing or refusing means losing the prize.

**Your submission**

- You keep ownership.
- You give the Sponsor a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable, **irrevocable** license to host, reproduce, display, distribute and make derivative works of it for running, judging, documenting and promoting the hackathon and the Sponsor's programs.
- You promise it's your original work or you have the rights, it respects third-party rights and open-source licenses, and it contains no malicious code, unlawful content or third-party confidential information.

**Prizes**

- Non-transferable.
- You pay any taxes, and may need to complete tax forms such as W-9 or W-8BEN.
- Prizes unclaimed within 30 days of notification may go to someone else or not be awarded.

**Disqualification**

The Sponsor can disqualify an entrant or take back a prize for breaking the rules, false information, failed verification, bots or multiple accounts, tampering, or abusive or unlawful behavior. Its decisions are final.

**General**

The Sponsor can modify, suspend or end the hackathon if it can't run as planned. US law governs.

## Unclear or inconsistent on the official pages

Ask in Discord if any of these affect you.

- **Track 3 winners:** the prize says the top 20 pull requests win, but the judging section says "the 10 most useful".
- **Prize total:** listed cash adds up to $6,500 ($2,000 + $2,000 + $2,000 + $500). The page doesn't break down the rest of the $10,000.
- **Open source:** the page calls Switch open source. The repo's license is Apache 2.0 with a modified Commons Clause, and the docs say "source-available".
- **Agent protocol:** the page says server-sent events. The Switch docs say SSE is deprecated, so use WebSocket for a custom agent.
- **Public repo:** implied by "clone the repo", but not stated.
- **Demo video:** no length limit is listed on these pages.
- **Telegram:** some page sections leave it out, but the FAQ says it can be connected.
