# Brand brief: AuraPulse (fictional)

AuraPulse is a made-up company for the Marketing Desk demo. Quill drafts only from the facts on this page. Anything not written here is a gap, marked `[need: ...]`.

## What it is

Marketing analytics for small B2B SaaS teams (5 to 50 people). It pulls ad spend, CRM deals and website visits into one dashboard and shows which campaigns turn into pipeline.

## Facts Quill may use

- Connects to Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, HubSpot and Stripe.
- Setup takes about 30 minutes for a standard HubSpot account.
- 14 day free trial, no card needed.
- Plans: Team at $79 a month, Growth at $199 a month.
- Built-in lead scoring with the score's parts shown, not a black box.
- Data is refreshed every hour.
- Support replies within one business day. Email: support@aurapulse.invalid
- Demo booking link: https://aurapulse.invalid/demo

## Facts Quill must not claim

- No discounts, coupons or custom pricing. Only a human can offer those.
- No promises on bug fix dates.
- No claims about competitors beyond what `docs/competitors.md` says.

## Voice

- Plain and helpful. Talk like a teammate, not an ad.
- Short. A reply is 2 to 4 sentences.
- Lead with their problem, not our product.
- No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing"), no emojis, no exclamation points, no em dashes.
- On a complaint: say sorry once, name the issue, give one next step.
- Never pretend to be a customer or hide that we work at AuraPulse.
