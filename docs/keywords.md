# Keywords Pulse listens for

| Group | Keywords | Usually means |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | AuraPulse, @aurapulse | Complaint, praise or question about us |
| Buying intent | marketing analytics tool, attribution, lead scoring, campaign ROI | Possible lead |
| Competitors | SignalNest, FunnelFox, MetricBloom | Competitor intel |

In the demo Pulse reads `samples/mentions.json` only. Live listening (for example with `SERPER_API_KEY` or `X_BEARER_TOKEN`) is not built. The sample file is what runs.
