# MOTION

Read before animating or checking anything. Written with `skills/keeper/brand-intake.md`. No reference frames exist yet, so this comes from `brand/brand-kit.md`, `skills/quill/formats.md` and `skills/cut/launch-video.md`. ASK ME marks what those files do not say.

## 1. Colours
- Night navy `#1B1F3B`: background of almost every frame.
- Paper `#F4EFE6`: text and cards.
- Ember `#FF6B4A`: one thing per frame only, the action or the key word.
- Mist `#8A8FA8`: secondary text.
- Captions: white on a navy box.

## 2. Type
- Titles and hooks: Space Grotesk 700, line height 1.05, letter spacing -0.02em.
- Everything else: Inter 400, line height 1.5.
- Captions: Inter 800, 64px, at most 4 words per line, key word in Ember.
- Title sizes: ASK ME.

## 3. Timing
- Hold any text at least 1.5 seconds.
- Hold the CTA at least 3 seconds.
- One idea per frame.
- Voiceover: about 140 words a minute.
- Music: lo-fi, 70 to 85 BPM, no vocals, ducked under voiceover.

| Format | Default length | Default aspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| launch | 30s | 9:16 |
| explainer | 60s | 16:9 |
| ugc | 30s, up to 60s | 9:16 |
| faceless | 30s, a multiple of 10s | 9:16 |

A render must match the length and aspect in the script's Format line. Hard maximum for launch, explainer and faceless: ASK ME.

## 4. Movement
- Slow push-ins and soft fades.
- Text comes in with a soft fade. The hook may already be on screen at t=0, because `hyperframes check` needs a visible first frame.
- Typing appears only inside a product input.
- Small position moves, fade length and easing curve: ASK ME.
- Frame rate: 30fps, the HyperFrames render used so far. Brand preference: ASK ME.

## 5. Texture and finish
Flat colour on night navy. Grain and other texture: ASK ME.

## 6. Safe areas
- 16:9: keep text inside the central 90 percent.
- 9:16: keep text out of the top 10 percent and the bottom 20 percent.

## 7. Never
1. Whip pans.
2. Glitch effects.
3. Glow.
4. Bounce.
5. Gradient text.

## 8. Done right
A 30s faceless short at 9:16:
- 0 to 3s: dark phone on night navy, hook above it in Paper, one word in Ember.
- 3 to 10s: phone stays still, two lines fade in one at a time.
- 10 to 20s: phone screen lights up, the entry appears, one word in Ember.
- 20 to 30s: text cards fade in one at a time, then a soft fade to the wordmark and "Driftnote. Say it out loud."
