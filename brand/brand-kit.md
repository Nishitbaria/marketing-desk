# Driftnote brand kit

DEMO / SYNTHETIC. Driftnote and its creator are fictional. Replace every value here with your own before a real run. Every agent reads this file first. The front matter follows Google's DESIGN.md format (Apache-2.0), so `npx -y @google/design.md lint brand/brand-kit.md` checks it.

## Overview

- Creator: Mira Sol, solo indie developer. DEMO.
- Product: Driftnote, a voice journal app for iOS. You talk for one minute before bed, it writes the entry and finds the pattern in your week.
- Audience: people who want to journal but never stick with writing. 22 to 35, phone first, tired of productivity guilt.
- Platforms: YouTube (16:9), YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels (9:16), X.

## Facts agents may use

These are the only product claims allowed. Anything else is `[need: ...]`.

- Speak for up to 60 seconds, get a written entry.
- Weekly recap: the three moods and topics that came up most.
- Entries stay on the phone. No account needed.
- Free with 7 entries a week. Driftnote Plus removes the limit. [need: price]
- Launch date: [need: date]

## Voice

- Tone: calm, honest, a little funny.
- Talk to one person, at night, at a low volume.
- Phrases we use: "say it out loud", "your week, written down", "no streaks, no guilt".
- Sample line that sounds right: "You don't need a new habit. You need one minute and a pillow."

## Colors

Night navy (`primary`) is the background of almost every frame. Paper (`secondary`) is text and cards. Ember (`accent`) is for one thing per frame only: the action or the key word. Mist (`neutral`) is for secondary text.

## Typography

Space Grotesk for titles and hooks. Inter for everything else. Captions are Inter 800, white on a navy box, max 4 words per line, key word in Ember.

## Layout & Spacing

- 16:9 safe area 90 percent. 9:16 keeps text out of the bottom 20 percent and top 10 percent.
- One idea per frame. Hold text at least 1.5 seconds.

## Motion and sound

- Slow push-ins and soft fades. No whip pans, no glitch effects.
- Music: lo-fi, 70 to 85 BPM, no vocals. Duck under voiceover.
- Voiceover: warm, unhurried, about 140 words a minute.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do show the phone screen with a real-looking entry.
- Do end every video on the logo wordmark and "Driftnote. Say it out loud."
- Do talk to one person as "you".
- Do name the real numbers plainly: up to 60 seconds, 7 free entries a week.
- Do use one of our phrases at least once in a script.
- Don't show faces in the faceless short.
- Don't use stock "person journaling in a cafe" shots.
- Don't promise outcomes like better sleep or less anxiety.
- Don't use guilt, streaks or urgency to push a download.
- Don't state a price, date or user count that is not in the facts above.

## Banned words

- "revolutionary"
- "game changer"
- "unlock"
- "supercharge"
- "10x"
- "AI-powered" as a headline

Also banned: any health or therapy claim, and any number we do not have.
