**1. Format**

Launch video, 30s, 9:16. Defaults, since the ask left these open: Shorts, Reels and TikTok, with the creator on camera as narrator. Spoken script is 68 words at the brand pace of 140 words a minute.

1. That journal by your bed? Mostly empty.
